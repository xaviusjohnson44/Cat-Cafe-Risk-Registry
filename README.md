# Purrfect Brew Cat Café Risk Registry
A demonstration of a Risk Registry made for a fictional cozy cat cafe!

<a href="https://github.com/xaviusjohnson44/Cat-Cafe-Risk-Registry/blob/main/Risk_Register_Cat_Cafe.xlsx">"Cat Café Risk Register"</a>

It demonstrates my ability to identify, evaluate, and score the likelihood and impact of potential threats, and to produce a simple risk register to track how those risks are handled. 

## Executive Summary

This assessment identified 13 cybersecurity risks affecting
Purrfect Brew Cat Café, a fictional small business that
operates a customer loyalty program, adoption management
system, guest Wi-Fi network, and cloud-based booking platform.
The highest-rated risk was phishing attacks against employee
email accounts (Risk Score: 9). Additional high-priority risks
included customer database breaches, adoption record leakage,
and ransomware attacks.
Recommended controls include multifactor authentication (MFA),
security awareness training, network segmentation, encryption,
strict access controls, and routine system backups.
